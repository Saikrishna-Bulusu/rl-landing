The obstacle flight, re-flown with three changes you asked about. First, it holds the 75 metre cruise until 90 metres from the pad instead of starting down 135 metres out; there the local 3D planner takes over and brings it down. Second, the blue ribbon is now drawn from the drone itself: before, it was drawn where PX4 believed the drone was, two to seven metres off, so the drone never seemed to follow it. Third, the planner no longer twitches: it used to re-join its old path at a point GeoAnchor's corrections had moved, and the command swung by up to 90 degrees. On the way down it meets a 58 metre crane nobody mapped; the depth camera wraps it in red cubes and the planner steps 14 metres to the side, passing at 6.7 metres. Honest note: the first re-flight hit the crane. With the new, higher approach the crane came into view only two and a half seconds out, and each plan took two seconds, so the answer came too late. A faster search, weighted A-star, brought a plan down to a tenth of a second, and the next flight passed cleanly. It landed 4 centimetres from the centre with GPS denied for 117 seconds.
