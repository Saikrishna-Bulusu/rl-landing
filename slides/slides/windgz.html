The same test on the real flight software. PX4 flies in Gazebo, twenty yards, four wind strengths, and every pilot meets exactly the same gusts, because the wind restarts from its seed for each flight. At 18 metres per second the rule lands none. The two flying learners land six and five, with no crashes, much like the twin said. The deciding learner lands fifteen of twenty, fourteen of them in yards where the rule failed, which is the gain carrying over. But three of its twenty flights crashed. In the twin that learner crashed about three percent of the time; in Gazebo it is fifteen. So the landing skill transfers and the safety only partly. Finding out what those three crashes have in common is the next job before anyone should trust the decider.
