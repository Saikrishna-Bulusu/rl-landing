Landings are flat and unordered — 9, 10, 8. RoMa has 39% less error and lands fewest.

The point-mass study agreed independently: rank corr(error, delivered) = +0.40, the wrong sign.

The marker absorbs the error. Once the pad is seen the drone flies to the pad, whether the fix was 7 m or 14 m wrong. Error only buys search time, and the battery has slack.
