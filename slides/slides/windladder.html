The scorecard in the twin. Each point is 1,024 landings in gusty wind from the same starting yards and the same gusts for every pilot. Orange is today's rule: fine up to 12 metres per second, 78 percent at 15, and at 18 it lands only 6 percent, because it keeps waiting for a calm moment and gives up. Green is the flying learner: it keeps the rule's decisions but adds a push against the gusts. Eight separate training runs all agree: about half the landings at 18 and almost no crashes, it gives up rather than crash. Blue is the deciding learner: it also chooses when to go down. It lands 87 percent at 18 and 64 at 20. The price is the right panel: it crashes more, 3 percent at 18 and 12 at 20, and the three seeds differ quite a lot. The lever that made it work was pricing a crash properly: with the crash penalty at minus 15 it crashed 12 percent at 18 metres per second, at minus 100 only 2 to 3 percent. Two single-seed variants, one also pricing hard landings, one pricing crashes at minus 300, reached 94 percent at 18; they need more seeds before I believe them.
